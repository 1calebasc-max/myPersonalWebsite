The Problems I Care About

Education Inequality

Many students miss out on valuable opportunities because they don't know about free programs, scholarships, and educational resources.

My Solution

The Access Code helps students discover free programs, scholarships, and educational opportunities based on their interests and grade level.

About Me

I created The Access Code because I believe technology should be used to solve real-world problems. My goal is to make educational opportunities and community resources easier to discover so more people can get the help they need.
